How to read it

always standsalways hits H = usually hits, S = usually stands an expert would do the opposite

Rows are the fly's total, columns the dealer's card. An expert's chart is a staircase: hit low totals, stand on high ones, and stand earlier when the dealer shows a weak card (2 to 6).

Measured in a real fly

  • The wiring. 4,406 neurons and 78,937 connections from the MaleCNS v1.0 connectome, each as strong as its measured synapse count.
  • Approach or avoid. Set by each output neuron's chemical transmitter, the rule found in fly experiments.
  • Where dopamine lands. 69% of reward synapses fall on avoid outputs and 91% of punishment synapses on approach outputs. That is why a win pulls the fly towards a choice and a loss pushes it away.

Invented for the game

  • Cards as smells. Flies smell, they do not read. Each situation and choice becomes a made-up smell across 57 smell channels.
  • Simple neurons. Activity levels, no spikes, and only the most driven 5% of Kenyon cells stay active.
  • The learning rule. The textbook idea (dopamine weakens the synapses that were just active), with my own numbers.
  • No real fly was involved, and this does not show that flies can play cards.

    What a fly does with each hand

    F = usually folds C = calls, K = checks R = bets or raises

    Rows are how good the fly's cards are; columns are the street and whether the fly can check, faces a bet, or faces a raise. A sensible player raises at the top, folds at the bottom when it costs something, and gets careful when raised.

    Measured in a real fly

    • Each fly's brain. All five run the same circuit as the blackjack fly, each with its own copy that learns only from its own wins and losses.
    • The learning. Every choice in a hand is paired with the chips won or lost, delivered as reward or punishment dopamine.

    Invented for the game

    • The strength smell. The page deals the hand out a few hundred times to see how often the fly's cards win, and gives the fly that as a smell in ten steps. The fly learns what to do about each smell.
    • The looks. Canton-S, white, yellow, ebony and Curly are real lab strains, used here only as costumes.
    • The rules. Limit betting keeps it to three choices. Stacks top up to 100 below 50 and bank winnings above 250, so nobody goes all in.
    • My settings. How loud the cards are in the smell, and how often a fly tries something other than its favourite choice.
